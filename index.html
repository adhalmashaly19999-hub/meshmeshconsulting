The leak

The money isn't lost in your ads. It's sitting in your spreadsheets.

Leads land in a sheet nobody opens

Facebook forms, website inquiries, WhatsApp messages. Each goes somewhere different, and by the time someone looks, the buyer has moved on.

Nobody knows who to call today

Without a list, the team calls whoever they remember. Follow-ups depend on memory, and memory loses.

You can't see what happened

You know what you spent on ads. You don't know how many leads were called, quoted or dropped.

Who it's for

Built for B2B teams that already get inquiries.

A good fit if

  • You sell to other businesses: factories, distributors, clinics, schools, developers, events.
  • Inquiries reach you every week, from ads, your website or WhatsApp.
  • Someone on your team is meant to call them back.
  • One lost deal costs you more than the system does.
  • You want to see the numbers, not hear about them.

Probably not a fit if

  • You don't get inquiries yet. Start with ads first, and we can help with that on its own.
  • You sell low-priced products that customers buy straight from your website.
  • Nobody on the team has time to make calls, and you don't plan to hire.

FAQ

Questions we get on every audit.

We already have a CRM. Why would we need you?

Most teams we meet have one. The question is whether anyone opens it every morning. In the audit we look at what you have. If your team uses it, we keep it: we work with any CRM that has an API, which most do, including HubSpot, Zoho and Salesforce, and connect your lead sources and follow-up to it. If nobody opens it, we set up one they will.

My sales team won't use another tool.

It's built around their day, on their phone: open it, see who to call, call, one tap to log it. There are no reports to type. And you can see who is using it, so it doesn't quietly get abandoned.

Do you only build CRMs?

No. We also build websites, run Meta ads, make video and set up automation. The CRM is usually where we start, because it makes everything else measurable: you can see which website form or campaign turned into a deal.

How long until it's running?

It depends on how many lead sources and old sheets you have. You get a go-live date with the quote, before anything starts. Your old leads are imported, so your team starts with a full list on day one.

What do you need from us?

Access to your lead sources, such as your Facebook page and website forms, the lead sheets you already have, your price list for quotes, and an hour with whoever runs sales.

Is our data safe?

Your leads stay yours. Each client gets their own system, only your team can sign in, and you can have a full export of your data whenever you ask.

Do you work outside Egypt?

Yes. We work in English and Arabic over video calls, and we've worked with teams in Egypt and the US.

What happens after the audit?

You keep the map and your top three leaks either way. If it's a fit, we send a fixed quote for the build. There's no pressure to sign.
