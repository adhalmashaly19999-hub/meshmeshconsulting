FAQ

Questions we get on every audit.

Do we need technical people on our team?

No. We build and set it up, train the people who will use it, and stay on to fix what gets in the way. Your team needs to use it, not code it.

Where should we start?

With the free audit. Most businesses have one place where AI pays off fast, often the sales follow-up or a report someone builds by hand every week. We start there, then add the next piece.

Is our data safe?

Your data stays yours. Each client gets their own system, only your team can sign in, and you can have a full export of your data whenever you ask.

We already have a CRM. Why would we need you?

Most teams we meet have one. The question is whether anyone opens it every morning. If your team uses it, we keep it: we work with any CRM that has an API, which most do, including HubSpot, Zoho and Salesforce, and connect your lead sources and follow-up to it. If nobody opens it, we set up one they will.

My team won't use another tool.

That's why we build around their day, on their phone, and train them on it. And you can see who is using it, so it doesn't quietly get abandoned.

Is the course the same as working with you?

No. In the course you learn to build things yourself. In a project, we build and run them for you. You can start with either.

Do you work outside Egypt?

Yes. We work in English and Arabic over video calls, and we've worked with teams in Egypt and the US.

What happens after the audit?

You keep the map either way. If it's a fit, we send a fixed quote and a go-live date for the first piece. There's no pressure to sign.
