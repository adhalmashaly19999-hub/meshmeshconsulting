The leak

The money isn't lost in your ads. It's sitting in your spreadsheets.

Leads land in a sheet nobody opens

Facebook forms, website inquiries, WhatsApp messages. Each goes somewhere different, and by the time someone looks, the buyer has moved on.

Nobody knows who to call today

Without a list, the team calls whoever they remember. Follow-ups depend on memory, and memory loses.

You can't see what happened

You know what you spent on ads. You don't know how many leads were called, quoted or dropped.

What we found when we opened one factory's lead sheets

882

leads from paid ads, spread across 8 spreadsheets

91%

had not been contacted in 60 days or more

EGP 57.9M

of inquiries sitting untouched*

*Each lead's minimum requested quantity multiplied by the client's lowest item price, about US$1.1M. A floor on what was asked for, not a sales forecast.

The system

One place for every lead. One list for every morning.

A sales CRM set up around how your team already works, on their phones, in English and Arabic.

01

Every lead, in seconds

Facebook lead forms, your website and your old spreadsheets feed one CRM. A new inquiry reaches your team's phones seconds after it is submitted.

02

A call list every morning

New inquiries, follow-ups due, and the cold leads worth another try, in order. Nobody decides who to call. They just start.

03

Call, then one tap

Call or WhatsApp straight from the lead. When they come back to the app, one tap records what happened and sets the next step.

04

Follow-ups that don't rely on memory

Every outcome schedules the next touch. Leads that go quiet come back onto the list instead of disappearing.

05

Quotes tied to the lead

Items, quantities and prices sit on the lead, so your pipeline is counted in money, not guesses.

06

You see everything

Calls made, leads priced and deals won, per person and per week, from your phone.
